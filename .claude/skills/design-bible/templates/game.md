# Game template

Topic id · title — guiding question(s). Group headings become `topic.group`.
Ask in this order during genesis. Users can add topics (their Xs and Ys); keep the ids stable.

## Vision
- `pitch` · Pitch — In one sentence: what is it, and why would someone play it over something else?
- `fantasy` · Player Fantasy — Who does the player get to be or feel like? What's the emotion to deliver?
- `pillars` · Pillars — Which 3 rules settle every design argument? What will you refuse to add?
- `audience` · Audience — Who is it for? What do they play now, and when or where do they play?
- `references` · References — What 2–3 games, films or books is it like, and how is it different from each?

## Play
- `core-loop` · Core Loop — What does the player do over and over, in 3–5 steps? Why does finishing one loop make them start the next?
- `mechanics` · Core Mechanics — What are the verbs (jump, build, trade…)? Which one is the star?
- `controls` · Controls & Feel — Which input device? What should the main verb feel like (weighty, snappy, floaty)?
- `goals` · Goals — What is the player trying to do in the moment, in a session, and across the whole game?
- `challenge` · Challenge — What makes it hard? How does difficulty grow, and is it skill, knowledge or resources?
- `failure` · Failure & Recovery — What happens when the player fails? What do they lose, and how fast are they back in?
- `progression` · Progression — What grows over time: the character, the player's skill, unlocks, the story?
- `economy` · Rewards & Economy — What does the player earn and spend? What are the sources and sinks?

## World
- `setting` · Setting — Where and when does it take place? What's the one striking thing about the world?
- `narrative` · Story — How much story, and how is it told (cutscenes, environment, systems)? How does it begin and end?
- `characters` · Characters — Who is the player character? Who matters besides them?
- `structure` · World & Level Structure — Is it linear, hub-based, open or procedural? Roughly how many levels or areas?
- `opposition` · Opposition — What pushes back: enemies, puzzles, time, other players?

## Presentation
- `art` · Art Direction — Describe it in three words. What's the reference image? 2D or 3D, and what camera?
- `audio` · Audio & Music — What does it sound like? What should audio tell the player that visuals don't?
- `ui` · UI & HUD — What must always be on screen? Diegetic or overlay?
- `camera` · Camera — Where is the camera, and who controls it?

## Experience
- `onboarding` · Onboarding — How does a new player learn the core verb in the first 5 minutes without a wall of text?
- `session` · Session Shape — How long is a typical session, and where are the natural stopping points?
- `replay` · Replayability — Why come back after the credits, or tomorrow?
- `accessibility` · Accessibility — Who could be locked out (motor, vision, hearing, cognitive), and what's the baseline commitment?

## Production
- `platform` · Platform & Tech — Which platforms and engine? Are there hard technical constraints?
- `scope` · Scope & MVP — What's the smallest version that proves the core loop is fun? Team size and timeline?
- `business` · Business Model — Premium, free-to-play, or a jam/portfolio piece? Does monetization touch design?
- `risks` · Risks — What could kill this project? What's the riskiest assumption, and how do you test it first?
